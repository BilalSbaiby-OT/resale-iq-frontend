description: What is waiting on the founder, and nothing else
allowed-tools: Read, Grep
---
Read `docs/company/APPROVALS.md` and list **only** the unchecked `- [ ]` items.

For each, give in one or two lines:
- what is blocked,
- what it costs the company while it waits,
- and the single action that clears it.

Do not restate resolved items, do not summarise the file, and do not add new items — this command
reports the founder's inbox, it does not fill it. If the inbox is empty, say so in one line.

If an item has been open long enough that its evidence may have gone stale, say that too. A gate
that was measured a week ago may be asking the founder to decide something that already changed.
