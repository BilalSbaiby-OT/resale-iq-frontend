   visitors total — the honest version of this number right now is "we don't have enough real,
   non-probe search volume to know our coverage rate." Second (A13, the good-news item in §1): a
   real chunk of the shortfall is a wrong-constant bug, not data scarcity — data-scientist's
   replication puts the *fixed* demand-side number at 81.1% all-time (n=180) once that ships,
   against 62.8% today on the same population. **The 59.1%/62.8% numbers don't agree with each
   other because they're computed on different windows and populations (7-day probe-contaminated

   competing truths.** The supply-side reading is currently the cleanest: **43.0% of the models we
